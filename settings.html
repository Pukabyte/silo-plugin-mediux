MediUX artwork settings

Choose how Mediux ranks creators and keeps season posters and title cards consistent.

Creator priority

Creators at the top take priority. Use the arrows to set their order.

      Artwork selection

      Creator priority first keeps one set from the highest-priority available creator, choosing that creator's set with the most artwork. Complete set first can choose a lower-priority creator to cover every season or episode. Missing artwork follows your gap setting. Posters and title cards can use different sets.
